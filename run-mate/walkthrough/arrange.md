## Buttons on each row

Point at a row in the script list and small buttons appear on the right.

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| ↑ ↓ | moves the script up or down |
| ☆ | makes it a favorite: listed first, first in the status bar |
| 👁 | hides it; it moves to **Hidden** at the bottom of the list |
| terminal | runs it with options (next step) |

What you arrange here is saved for this workspace only.

Too many buttons in the status bar? Click the gear at the top of the list and choose **Script buttons shown**.
