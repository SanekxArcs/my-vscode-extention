## Groups

1. Open the script list and click **+** at the top.
2. Tick the scripts that belong together, for example `dev` of `web` and `dev` of `api`.
3. Give the group a name, for example `dev all`.

A `dev all` button appears in the status bar. One click starts every script of the group, another click stops them.

### Share it with your team

Click the people icon on the group's row. RunMate moves the group to `.vscode/settings.json`. Commit that file, and everyone who uses RunMate in this repository gets the same button.
